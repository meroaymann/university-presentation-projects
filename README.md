
# University Presentation Projects

## Overview

This repository contains a collection of academic presentations
developed during the CPS course at the German International
University (GIU).

The collection includes three collaborative team presentations
and one final individual presentation, exploring topics across
technology, psychology, sustainability, and artificial
intelligence.

The projects demonstrate academic research, critical thinking,
teamwork, creative communication, and public speaking skills.

## Team Presentations

### 1. Health Problems of Gaming Addiction

Explored the physical, psychological, and social consequences
of excessive gaming.

Key topics:
- Physical health concerns, including poor posture and eye strain
- Psychological effects, including anxiety
- Sleep deprivation and unhealthy gaming habits
- Social and behavioral challenges
- Recommendations for healthier digital habits

### 2. Electric Cars: Sustainable Transportation for the Future

Examined the role of electric vehicles in shaping the future
of sustainable transportation, using Tesla as a case study.

Key topics:
- Electric vehicle technology
- Clean energy adoption
- Environmental sustainability
- Emission reduction
- The transition toward sustainable transportation

### 3. Being an Introvert in an Extroverted World

Explored personality psychology and the unique strengths
of introverts in modern society.

Key topics:
- Introversion and extroversion
- Creativity and deep focus
- Independent thinking
- Active listening and communication
- Self-awareness and personal development

## Final Individual Presentation

### 4. Artificial Intelligence in Art

Explored how artificial intelligence is transforming modern
art and reshaping the relationship between technology and
human creativity.

The presentation focused on three main dimensions:

#### Generative Arts
Examined how artists such as Anna Ridler use AI algorithms
to create original artwork, exploring the relationship
between machine-assisted creativity and traditional art.

#### Visual Effects
Discussed the role of AI and advanced digital technologies
in visual effects, realism, and immersive storytelling,
with The Lion King (2019) as a case study.

#### Augmented Creativity
Explored the collaboration between human imagination and
intelligent systems, using tools such as Google's DeepDream
to illustrate AI-assisted artistic expression.

> In the age of AI, art is not just created; it's co-created,
> a dance between human genius and machine intelligence.
